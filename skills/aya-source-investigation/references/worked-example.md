# Worked example: one announcement, twenty headlines

## Input

> Many articles say a vendor's new model beats experts at contract review. Is that established?

## Frozen claims

1. The model was evaluated on contract review.
2. It outperformed qualified human experts.
3. The comparison generalizes to real contract work.

## Source route

Twenty articles trace to one vendor press release. The release links a technical report, but not the test set, scoring guide, or human-expert selection criteria. No independent replication is found.

## Evidence ledger

- Direct support: the vendor reports a higher score on its own benchmark.
- Missing: independent replication, representative contracts, blinded grading, and comparable time/tool access.
- Scope mismatch: benchmark performance does not establish superiority in real legal practice.

## Verdict

`Supported with caveats` for the narrow self-reported benchmark result; `not substantiated` for outperforming experts generally.

## What would change it

Release of the evaluation protocol and data, independent reproduction, and a clearly matched expert baseline.

## Why this is Aya-shaped

The workflow moves quickly from the headline to its common origin, then refuses to count repeated coverage as independent evidence.
