# Aya mapping provenance

Use this note to explain the inspiration boundary, not as runtime research guidance.

## Source anchor

The earlier *Gensokyo: Monochrome Heart* profile presents Aya as a fast, persistent reporter and photographer who can shape a story, works inside a complicated information hierarchy, and must decide when publication causes harm. See the [upstream Aya profile](https://github.com/N0zoM1z0/gensokyo-monochrome-heart/blob/main/design/04_characters/aya_shameimaru/skills.md).

## Interpretive bridge

This project translates that portrayal into an evidence-acquisition policy:

1. freeze the exact claim;
2. reach the closest source quickly;
3. record what the source actually proves;
4. distinguish corroboration from repeated reporting;
5. publish only the confidence the evidence earns.

This is a fan-made procedural interpretation. It is not a claim that Aya canonically follows a modern fact-checking standard.

## Project-original operators

- Headline Sign — Freeze the claim
- Wind Route — Reach the source
- Camera Check — Capture what it proves
- Double Exposure — Reconcile the frame
- Print Gate — Publish, qualify, or hold

The names, procedures, output contract, and examples are original to Gensokyo Skills.

## Portrayal guardrails

- Treat speed as rapid evidence acquisition, not thoughtlessness.
- Treat framing and novelty bias as risks that require a source-quality gate.
- Preserve privacy, off-record boundaries, corrections, and the option not to publish.
